We Design Objects
That Age Well

NOVA/FORM is a studio creating contemporary objects for people who appreciate design, craft, and the quiet beauty of well-considered things.

Philosophy

Less, but better.

We believe in removing the unnecessary until only the essential remains. Every NOVA/FORM product begins with a question: what can we take away? The result is objects that feel inevitable — as though no other form could exist.


Our design process is deliberately slow. We prototype extensively, test obsessively, and refine until each piece meets our standard: honest materials, precise proportions, and a form that improves with age.

Materials

Honest materials,
honestly used.

We work with travertine, brass, oak, linen, stoneware, and other materials that develop character over time. We never disguise one material as another. What you see is what you get — and what you get only gets better.

Craftsmanship

Made by people
who care.

Every NOVA/FORM product is manufactured by skilled artisans and small workshops. Our ceramics are hand-formed in Kyoto. Our brass is machined in Birmingham. Our stone is cut in Tuscany. We know every maker by name.

Sustainability

The most sustainable product is the one you keep.

We design for longevity, not trends. Every NOVA/FORM object is built to last decades, not seasons. We use recyclable packaging, carbon-offset shipping, and partner only with manufacturers who share our commitment to responsible production.
